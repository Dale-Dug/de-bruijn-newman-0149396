## Bundled numerical runtime

with python-flint 0.8.0 and its bundled native libraries. Its metadata records
`MIT AND LGPL-3.0-or-later`. The archive includes the existing licences and
notices for python-flint, FLINT 3.4.0, GMP 6.3.0 and MPFR 4.2.2. Those texts govern
their respective components and are not replaced by this summary.

The paths are under the embedded parent source:
`base/runtime/python_flint-0.8.0.dist-info/licenses/`.
