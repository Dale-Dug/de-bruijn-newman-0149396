# Review and correction requests

This preprint is open for independent scrutiny, not presented as having received external mathematical approval. The manuscript and foundations specify the actual assertions; the numerical evidence is not a substitute for the analytic proofs.

The principal review targets are:

1. The larger-height approximation used by the initial probes: domains, source normalisation, Gaussian and Riemann–Siegel remainders, and cutoff changes.
2. The shared-phase value–derivative inequality, complete exceptional-pair debit, and derivative-error transfer.
3. The correlated translated-probe surplus: unequal source coefficients, denominators, source seams, and prevention of double counting.
4. The localisation wall and global first-contact argument, including collisions and spatial/time boundaries.
5. The correspondence between the written inequalities, interval producers, exact comparison and recorded endpoint.

For a numerical report, give the operating system, architecture, Python version, exact command, source identity, failure output and the earliest failing check. Do not replace an unresolved interval sign with an ordinary floating-point value.

For a mathematical objection, quote the exact statement and distinguish a false conclusion from an insufficient proof or an implementation discrepancy. Corrections should preserve earlier archival versions and describe precisely what changes.

No post-publication external review or new replay is implied by this file.
